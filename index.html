Getting Here

Arrival & Local Transit

Our celebrations span Kundara (Kollam) for the Engagement and Chengaroor (near Thiruvalla & Mallappally) for the Wedding, both easily reached by air, rail, and road.

  • Airports: Trivandrum International Airport (TRV, ~1.5–2 hrs to Kundara; ~3 hrs to Chengaroor) and Cochin International Airport (COK, ~2.5–3 hrs to Chengaroor) offer frequent domestic and Gulf connections.
  • By Rail: Kundara (KUV) and Kollam Junction (QLN, ~25 mins) serve the Engagement venue; Thiruvalla Railway Station (TRVL, ~25 mins) is the nearest railhead to Chengaroor Church.
  • Local Transfers: Airport pickups and cab coordination between Kundara, Thiruvalla, and Chengaroor are available on request—share your arrival plans in your RSVP note.
Hospitality & Lodging

Recommended Stays

Whether you are joining us in Kundara, Chengaroor, or for the full week of celebrations, warm Kerala hospitality and comfortable town or lakeside stays are nearby.

  • Engagement (Kundara & Kollam): Ashtamudi lakeside resorts and modern town hotels in Kollam sit 20–25 minutes from YMCA Auditorium, Arumurikkada.
  • Wedding (Thiruvalla & Mallappally): Boutique town hotels and quiet riverside heritage homes across Thiruvalla and Mallappally are 15–30 minutes from St George Orthodox Church, Chengaroor.
  • Stay Assistance: Let us know your check-in dates and guest count when you RSVP—our families will gladly share nearby stay options and help arrange rooms.
