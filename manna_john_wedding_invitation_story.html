Travel, Stay & Attire

Getting Here

Arrival & Local Transit

Central Kerala is well connected by air, rail, and scenic state highways, making arrival straightforward whether you are flying in or traveling across Kerala.

  • Airports: Cochin International Airport (COK, ~2.5 hrs drive) and Trivandrum International Airport (TRV, ~3 hrs drive) both offer direct domestic and Gulf connections.
  • By Rail: Thiruvalla Railway Station (TRVL) sits just 10 minutes from our venues with frequent express trains.
  • Local Cabs: Airport transfers and local cab coordination are available on request—simply mention your arrival details in your RSVP note.
Seasonal Dressing

July Monsoon & Attire Note

July brings lush emerald greenery, cool breezes, and refreshing monsoon showers to Central Kerala.

  • Festive Palette: Classic Kerala Kasavu sarees and mundus, light breathable silks, pastel linen suits, and elegant formal wear fit the mood effortlessly.
  • Monsoon Comfort: Choose breathable fabrics and comfortable footwear for church and hall transitions, and keep a compact umbrella handy between stops.
Hospitality & Lodging

Recommended Stays

Curated boutique and heritage stays in Thiruvalla and Changanassery are located within 10–15 minutes of both venues, with family room blocks held until May 2027.

  • Thiruvalla & Changanassery: Quiet riverside heritage homes and modern town hotels close to the church and reception hall.
  • Family Room Blocks: Let us know your check-in dates when you RSVP and we will gladly share booking codes and local assistance.
